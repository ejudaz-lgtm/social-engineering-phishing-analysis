# Phishing Analysis 2

## Platform
BlueTeamLabs Online

## Objective
To investigate a suspected phishing email and identify indicators of compromise (IOCs).

## Analysis Performed
- Email header inspection
- URL reputation analysis
- Attachment and link examination
- Domain and IP verification

## Indicators of Phishing
- Mismatched sender address
- Malicious URLs disguised as legitimate links
- Shortened or obfuscated URLs
- Poor email formatting and grammar

## Social Engineering Techniques
- Impersonation of a trusted organization
- Fear-based messaging
- Call-to-action prompting quick response

## Defensive Findings
- Header fields reveal abnormal mail routing
- Domain registration history indicates recent creation
- Links redirect to credential harvesting pages

## Mitigation Strategies
- Implement SPF, DKIM, and DMARC
- Block malicious domains at email gateway
- Educate users to verify links and senders
- Incident response playbooks for phishing

## Conclusion
This challenge reinforces the importance of email analysis
and proactive phishing defense in organizational security.
